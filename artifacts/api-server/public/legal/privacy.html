Habiganize Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Habiganize (“we”, “our service”) is a habit-building app with companion pets, available at habitganizer.tech and in our mobile apps. This policy explains what personal data we process when you use Habiganize.

Who we are

Habiganize is operated as a consumer web and mobile product. For privacy requests, contact us via the support page.

Authentication

Sign-in and account management are handled by Clerk (clerk.com), an authentication provider. Clerk processes identifiers such as email address and related account metadata according to Clerk’s privacy policy.

Data we process

  • Account information: Provided through Clerk for sign-in (for example email and profile fields you choose to share).
  • Habit data: Habits you create, schedules, completions, moods, notes, and related timestamps.
  • Companion / rewards state: In-app progression tied to your account (for example coins, pets, care actions).
  • Health & grocery (optional): Entries you add in those features, if you use them.
  • Device notifications: If you opt in on mobile, reminders may use platform notification services.
  • Payments: If you purchase coins, donate, or subscribe, payment processors (such as Stripe or Clerk Billing) handle card details; we receive confirmation and entitlement data, not full card numbers.
  • Feedback: Messages you send through in-app feedback.

How we use it

To operate Habiganize (sync habits, dashboards, reminders, companions, social features you enable), process purchases and subscriptions you request, prevent abuse, improve reliability, and meet legal obligations where required. We do not sell your personal data.

Hosting and security

Habiganize application data is stored on managed cloud infrastructure (including a PostgreSQL database and our API host). Traffic between the app and our API uses HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained while your account remains active unless you delete it through product features or contact support. You may delete your Clerk account using Clerk’s account tools; to request deletion of Habiganize application data, contact us through support.

Contact

For privacy requests, see our support page.
